Open it

  1. Open the link

    The tracker for senior leaders, or Staff entry for teachers.

  2. First time only: paste the flow link

    Into the yellow box at the top, then Connect. Senior leaders have it. It stays in that browser, so you won't be asked again on that computer.

  3. Enter the passphrase

    The staff passphrase for Staff entry, the SLT one for the tracker. Teachers then choose their name.

  • Install it as an appIn Edge, choose ⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app. In Chrome, use the install icon in the address bar. It opens in its own window, with an icon in the Start menu or Dock.
  • On an iPadIn Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Always the latest versionUpdates arrive by themselves the next time it's opened. There's nothing to download or copy into SharePoint.

Enter

  • LevelsType a level from FLS1 to 9S and press Enter to go down the class. = repeats the last drop, + and - move it a step, and you can paste a column from Excel. It saves as you go.
  • GCSE grades, with the other qualificationsEach GCSE subject has its own page: grade now, target and exam result, U to 9, with the status against target as you type. You can also enter every subject for a class at once.
  • BTEC, ASDAN, Functional Skills and Entry LevelStatus, units and grades at each data drop. A course that stands in for a subject, such as BTEC Science, is judged in its place.
  • Curriculum statementsSign off each statement as emerging, developing or secure, one pupil at a time or for the whole class.
  • Assessments and AAA pathwaysScores and reading ages, with thresholds to watch. Pathway updates are logged as they happen.

Progress

  • On track, ahead or behindLevels are measured against 0.8 of a level a year since joining, with too early shown until there's enough to judge. GCSE grades are compared with the target.
  • One status everywhereThe home page, pupil cards, reports and flags all give a pupil the same status for a subject.
  • Pupil pagesEach pupil's levels, GCSEs and qualifications, assessments, AAA pathway and notes. Curriculum statements show where they are and their next steps.
  • Data checksDuplicates, odd teaching groups, missing results and anything SharePoint refused, each with a way to put it right.
  • Export a data dropEvery pupil and subject, with the level or grade, the previous result and the progress since: up 2 steps, no change, down 1 grade. Qualifications show where the pupil is.

Staff entry

Teachers have their own file. It opens on their class and subject and shows only their pupils. It can't save anything outside that, and it keeps nothing on the computer.

  1. Open it from SharePoint

    Enter the staff passphrase and choose your name, once per computer.

  2. Type and press Enter

    Levels, GCSE grades, sign-offs and qualification updates save as you go.

  3. See it saved

    The chip at the top says All saved. A box with an orange edge hasn't reached SharePoint yet.

Your data

  • Kept in the school's SharePointThe tracker reads and writes the school's own SharePoint lists through the school's own Power Automate flow. Every request needs a passphrase.
  • Nothing on GitHubThis site and its repository hold code and documents only. The tracker runs in your browser and talks straight to the school's flow, so no data passes through GitHub. The flow's link is kept out, and the tests use a made-up school.
  • Safe when two people work at onceOnly the fields that changed are sent, and colleagues' sign-offs are merged rather than overwritten. A dropped connection loses nothing.

The tracker is made for one school's set-up: its SharePoint lists and its Power Automate flow. Without the school's flow link and passphrase it opens, but it can't reach any data.

Build it

  1. Add the flow link on your computer

    Put it in flow-url.txt next to build.js. Git ignores this file, so the link never reaches GitHub.

  2. Build both files

    node build.js out/release makes the SLT tracker and the Staff entry file from one source.

  3. Put them in SharePoint

    Replace both files in the school's dashboards folder. The in-school check takes about 12 minutes.

  4. Update the web version

    node tools/build-pages.js rebuilds app/ and staff/, always without the flow link. Commit and push, and GitHub Pages serves it.
